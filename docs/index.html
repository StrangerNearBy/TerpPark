TerpPark - UMD Parking Guide

TerpPark is a free tool for checking parking at the University of Maryland, College Park. Look up any UMD parking lot to see its restriction rules and whether it's open right now, find the nearest available lot or visitor parking garage to any building on campus, and browse an interactive map of every lot at College Park, MD.
